- Workspace settings: while the server has the app theme switch on (`APP_THEME_PIGMENT_ENABLED`), owners and
  admins find an **Appearance** card on the Themes page to choose between the Daybreak and Pigment looks of the
  dashboard, in English and Dutch. The choice is part of the workspace and is shown to its members only, never to
  respondents, and the activity log records the change. With the switch off nothing is shown or asked for.
