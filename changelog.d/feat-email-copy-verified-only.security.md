- The respondent's copy of an email action now goes only to a signed-in respondent, at the verified email of the
  account they submitted with (read when the copy is sent; an account that no longer exists gets nothing), and never
  to an address typed in an answer. Anonymous respondents (not signed in, a session whose email was not verified, or
  anyone who submits anonymously) get no copy: the log records "not verified" without any address, and that uses no
  rate-limit budget. The email action's copy setting is now a switch instead of an email question. The form builder
  says plainly that anonymous respondents get no copy and warns when the form does not ask respondents to sign in
  ("Require verified identity"); the privacy panel says the same.
