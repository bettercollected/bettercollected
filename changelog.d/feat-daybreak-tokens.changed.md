- **A new colour look for the creator app: Daybreak.** The dashboard, the form builder, settings, the Report page and sign-in now sit on a soft periwinkle ground with white cards. The blue you know stays the only button colour. The active menu item is blue on a blue tint, a chosen option or row has a 2 px blue edge plus a check, and statuses (Published, Draft, Verified, Failed) pair a dot with a word. Report charts use one blue for single-question bars and a six-colour set (blue, ochre, teal, red-orange, violet, slate) for donuts, each clearly visible on the white card. Published forms and the respondent pages look exactly the same as before.
